Biography

Simon Parkinson is a Professor of Cyber Security at Manchester Metropolitan University, where his research addresses the secure identification, authentication, and governance of people, devices, and autonomous systems. He is a member of the UK Government’s Cyber Security Advisory Board and of the Department for Science, Innovation and Technology (DSIT) College of Experts.

His expertise spans digital identity, access control, behavioural biometrics, operational technology security, cyber resilience, and the application of artificial intelligence to security analysis, compliance assessment, and risk management. The common thread is turning complex access and configuration data into evidence organisations can act on. More specifically, identifying weaknesses in access control systems, detecting cyber intrusions, and preventing unauthorised access using emerging biometric technologies. Professor Parkinson has led more than £2.7 million of externally funded research and knowledge exchange, supported by EPSRC, Dstl, Innovate UK, and industry partners.

He has supervised 22 research degrees to completion.
