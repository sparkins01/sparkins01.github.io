Simon Parkinson is a Professor of Cyber Security whose research sits at the intersection of artificial intelligence and cyber security. His work develops intelligent techniques that automate security expertise: analysing security event logs and access-control configurations, supporting digital forensic investigations, and building biometric and wireless-sensing authentication systems. He also has a long-standing interest in the security of connected and autonomous vehicles, intelligent transport and railway systems, and in using large language models to support security compliance for operational technology.

He spent over a decade at the University of Huddersfield, progressing from Research Fellow (2014) to Professor of Cyber Security (2022), where he was Director of the Centre for Cyber Security and Computer Science Research Co-ordinator. He is a member of the UK Government Cyber Security Advisory Board (GCAB) and the DSIT College of Experts, and Associate Editor of IET Intelligent Transport Systems. His PhD, from the University of Huddersfield, applied domain-independent automated planning to machine tool calibration.
