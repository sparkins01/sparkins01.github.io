Biography

Simon Parkinson is Professor of Cyber Security at Manchester Metropolitan University. His expertise spans cyber security, with a particular focus on identity and access. This involves establishing who, or what, is using a system, deciding what they should be allowed to do, and recognising when something has gone wrong. His work turns complex identity, access and configuration data into information organisations can act on. This ranges from authenticating people by how they behave to finding weaknesses in access control. Increasingly, this work extends to AI agents, helping to understand and better control their access. He has secured more than £3.1 million of research and knowledge exchange funding from EPSRC, Dstl, Innovate UK and industry.

He is a member of the UK Government’s Cyber Security Advisory Board and the DSIT College of Experts. He has supervised 25 research degrees to completion and has received four Supervisor of the Year awards.

He welcomes enquiries from prospective PhD students interested in identity, authentication and access control, including the security of AI agents.
